## Publishing from Studio

Open `studio/studio.html`, connect with your GitHub token, pick a section in the sidebar and press **Publish**. Your site updates 1–2 minutes later.

- **Posts and Notes:** long and short writing, with tags, drafts and pinning.
- **Learn topics:** one numbered square per concept. Every `## Heading` becomes a card.
- **Moments:** drop wildlife photos and each one is published as its own moment.
- **Sources and Research notes:** papers, books, journals, websites and videos, with highlights, questions, LaTeX, screenshots and pen pages.

## Writing tools

> [!TIP]
> Use `> [!TIP]`, `[!NOTE]`, `[!INFO]`, `[!WARNING]` or `[!DANGER]` for callouts.

- **Code:** add a language after the opening fence (` ```python `) for colors and a copy button.
- **Math:** `$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$` inline, or `$$ ... $$` on its own lines. Write `\$` for a literal dollar sign.
- **Diagrams:** a ` ```mermaid ` code block renders a flowchart.
- **Images and video:** drag and drop or paste. Use ` ![caption](youtube:VIDEO_ID) ` for YouTube.
- **Pen note:** write by hand, optionally on top of a pasted screenshot, and insert the pages into your note.
- Press **/** anywhere on the site to search. The ◐ button switches between light and dark.

## Research and copyright

PDFs are never uploaded. Link to the legal source instead (arXiv, a DOI, the publisher). Mark a source as **Copyrighted** when you only want to publish your own notes and short quotes.
